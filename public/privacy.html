Kitbasher workbench

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kitbasher is a local-first 3D assembly workbench maintained by Michael Pearce. We do not track your activity, run advertising, sell personal information, or use your models to train AI. You can use the workbench without creating an account.

Your models stay local by default

In normal local use, imported files, models, assemblies, and edits are processed on your device in your browser. They are not uploaded to us or to Cloudflare. Saved exports go to a location you choose. Optional cloud saving and shared sessions work differently, as explained below.

Browser storage, not tracking

Kitbasher uses browser storage, including local storage and IndexedDB, to remember preferences and keep recovery data on your device. Shared-session tabs also use session storage to keep a temporary session identifier. These are functional features, not advertising or tracking tools. We do not add analytics, tracking cookies, tracking pixels, or session recording.

You can remove this data by clearing Kitbasher's site data in your browser. Doing so removes preferences and recovery copies, so export any work you want to keep first. Browser storage is not a substitute for a saved backup. Files you export or save to Google Drive remain until you delete them there.

Cloudflare hosting

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website. To deliver pages and protect its network, Cloudflare processes technical request information, which may include your IP address, traffic routing information, and system configuration information. It may retain operational and security logs. This hosting traffic is separate from the model data processed locally in your browser.

Cloudflare handles this information under its Privacy Policy, which explains its processing, retention, international transfers, and privacy choices. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles.

Optional Google Drive saving

If available, Google Drive saving is optional. Opening the Drive dialog loads Google's sign-in service, which sends requests to Google. If you choose to connect, Kitbasher requests permission to access files you create with or open through the app, rather than unrestricted access to your Drive. Scene files and associated metadata are transferred directly between your browser and Google Drive for cloud saving and loading, not through a Kitbasher backend. Access tokens are held in memory, not stored in browser storage or scene files.

Google's services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Kitbasher's access in your Google Account and delete saved files in Google Drive. Local use does not require connecting Google.

Optional shared sessions and integrations

If you join a live, headset, or MCP-backed session, scene data and edits are exchanged with the session server and connected participants or tools. A server you run may make that data available to devices on your network. Only join sessions and connect tools you trust. Any external AI service or other integration you choose to use handles the information you share under its own policies; local-only processing does not apply to information you deliberately share this way.

Contact and external links

If you contact us or report an issue, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond or investigate. GitHub issues are public, so do not include personal information or private models. External services have their own privacy policies. For privacy questions or requests, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Kitbasher or its services change. The current version will be published here with an updated date.
